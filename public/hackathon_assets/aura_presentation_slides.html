The Real-World Financial Problem

CHALLENGE

1. Heavy Document Overhead

Annual reports (Form 10-K) exceed 100+ pages. Sifting through multi-column financial statements to extract Free Cash Flow, CapEx, or Net Income takes analysts hours of manual work.

2. Vector RAG Scrambles Tables

Standard text-chunk RAG breaks multi-year tables. Embedding models lose column alignment, causing AI tools to mix up FY2021, FY2022, and FY2023 figures.

3. GAAP Accounting Violations

Generic chatbots invent nonsensical metrics (e.g. calculating "gross margin" for commercial banks or insurance underwriters that have no cost-of-goods-sold).

4. Unusable Voice Latency

Traditional multi-hop cloud RAG architectures create 2.5 to 5.0 second latency delays, breaking natural conversational turn-taking on live voice calls.
